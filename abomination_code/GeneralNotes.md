1) An NPC will continue to sleep until the scheduled time for the NPC to wake up unless sleep is interrupted for an emergency or high priority task.

1b) [Low pri] an NPC may wake up early if they reach 100% energy before they are scheduled to wake up, and an NPC may sleep past their scheduled wake up time if they are not at least 65% energy when they are scheduled to wake up.

2) [Low pri] an NPC may eat early if they reach 10% hunger before they are scheduled to eat, and an NPC may eat late if they are not at least 60% hunger when they are scheduled to eat.

2a) Activities Related to Eating: traveling to the Kitchen or Dining room and consuming a meal should take less time than the alotted time for "Eating." Each NPC should prioritize eating a prepared meal during their eating time. An NPC will continue eating and finish their meal even if the alotted time for "Eating" has expired. If an NPC is in the process of eating when their scheduled eating time expires, they will finish their meal and then proceed to their next task. If an NPC finishes their meal before their scheduled eating time expires, if time permits, they will wash their dishes. If an NPC has exhausted all Activities Related to Eating with time remaining in their scheduled eating time, they will remain in an idle state until their scheduled eating time concludes. 

2b) Each NPC only consumes one meal during meal time. Consuming two prepared meals a day should more than satisfy any NPC's hunger needs and ensure the NPC never drops below 12% fullness. 

2c) If an NPC cannot find a prepared meal to consume at meal time, they should prepare a meal if they have the Cooking responsibility and ingredients are available. If they do not have the Cooking responsibility, they should eat a raw ingredient if available. If they cannot find a prepared meal or a raw ingredient, they should perform a task that will lead to them obtaining a prepared meal or a raw ingredient (gather eggs from the chicken coop, harvest produce from the fields or garden, butcher an animal, etc.).

3) if the NPC has exceeded any status limits such they the "Approach Critical Threshold" (<11% Fullness, >84% Digestion, <11% Energy, <11% Satisfaction), the appropriate task to address that need becomes a high priority task that overrides any other tasks the NPC may have except for an emergency response. Fullness-> eat an available meal; if not possible prepare an available meal; if not possible, eat a raw ingredient, if not possible, pass out. Digestion -> use toilet/washroom; if not possible, have bowel incident. Energy -> go take a nap, if not possible, pass out. Satisfaction -> go for a walk, attack a character or a creature, or pass out. 


  3a) if an NPC has multiple high priority assignments (e.g. the NPC approaches multiple critical thresholds at the same time), they should perform each high priority tasks in the chronological order in which the task was added to the high priority queue.
  3b) if an NPC reaches the point of "Exceeds Critical Threshold" (0% Fullness, 100% Digestion, 0% Energy, 0% Satisfaction), they face an increasing risk (starting at 1% per minute and increasing by 1% every 5 minutes thereafter) of emergency consequences: (passing out, bowel incident, panic attack, attacking another character, eating raw/spoiled food) if the need is not addressed. 
  3c) A bowel incident immediately resets the NPC's digestion to 0%. Passing out immediately resets the NPC's energy to 15% and the NPC stays asleep until reaching 70% energy. Panic attacks reset the NPC's satisfaction to 60% (but continuous panic attacks increase the risk of a complete mental breakdown or heart attack). 

4) Tasks assigned to NPCs by the player either by dragging an NPC to a room or using a Room's interface to assign an NPC to perform a task there, that task is assigned a normal priority level.

4a) Every time a new task is manually assigned, it is appended to the bottom/end of the normal priority task list.

4b) The player can manually rearrange assignments in each queue by dragging and dropping the tasks in the assignment list on the NPC's Status Card (visible when you click on an NPC from the Manor View).

4c) All assignments on an NPC’s status card can be canceled or reordered by the player, but there are three distinct lists: high priority, normal priority, and low priority. A high priority intent should never come before an emergency, a normal priority intent should never come before high priority or emergency, and a low priority intents should never come before normal priority, high priority, or emergency.

5) low priority work tasks are governed by the NPC's Responsibilities settings and character traits and include: clean room, cook meal, feed animals, tend the garden, water crops, care for crops, perform research.

5a) example Giles Work activities:

Collect eggs (Responsibility: Labor)
Guard the chickens (Responsibility: Labor)
Restore (any room that needs restoration) (Responsibility: Cleaning)

Till Field (Responsibility: Farming)
Fertilize Field (Responsibility: Farming)
Care for Crops (Responsibility: Farming)
Water Crops (Responsibility: Farming)
Stand at attention in the Entry (Responsibility: Labor)
